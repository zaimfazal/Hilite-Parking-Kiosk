A college demonstration of a smart parking system for HiLITE Mall. It simulates number-plate scanning, allocates a real bay with floor and zone directions, quotes fees at exit with a demo payment, and gives administrators a live dashboard.

Open the parking kiosk Admin dashboard

The app runs on a free server that sleeps when idle, so the first visit can take about a minute to wake up. Demo data starts fresh after each restart. Demo admin PIN: 1234.

Vehicle entry

Simulated plate scan, car or bike, EV charging request, and an instant bay allocation.

Directions and ticket

Floor, bay and zone signage with a simulated parking ticket.

Exit and fees

First 2 hours free; then ₹5 per hour for cars and ₹2 for bikes, with a demo payment.

Live dashboard

Occupancy, floor maps, peak hour, long-stay alerts, kiosk mode and demo reset.

Demonstration only. There is no real camera, printer or payment gateway, and no real money is collected. Built in Java with Spring Boot and Vaadin.
